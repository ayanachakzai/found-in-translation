# Motion Lab — final visual direction

## Scope

`/motion-lab` is an isolated physical-system prototype. It proves the archive,
recognition, story generation, receipt output and Arduino fallback sequence without
changing the main site, narrative, dataset, receipt archive or deployment flow.

## Environment

The world is a dark physical exhibition: a `#0c0d0f` charcoal room and a matte
`#181a1d` floor, joined by perspective and soft contact grounding rather than a
visible horizon. The canvas is opaque, has no CSS filter, overlay, blend mode,
vignette or fog. ACES Filmic tone mapping uses explicit `1.28` exposure and a
maximum DPR of `1.5`; the room is dark while the materials retain midtone detail.

## Zones and hierarchy

1. **Archive and training.** An old book is central in a warm archival pool. Owl,
   snake, rose, crown and star form a readable shallow arc: wisdom, greed, love,
   power and mysticism have physical markers rather than generic AI graphics.
2. **Recognition.** Star and rose move from the left toward a right-hand webcam.
   Its verified physical lens side faces left and slightly toward the viewer, so
   the opening is readable in a three-quarter view. The book and other objects
   recede to a quiet archive field. The printer remains outside the active frame.
3. **Retrieval and generation.** The centre clears. A few authentic project words
   travel from archive toward output, suggesting retrieval and recombination
   without pretending that a browser is training a model.
4. **Printing.** The black thermal printer is the dominant object. It has neutral
   key/rim separation, a visible slot and a warm 2700–3200K practical as the
   supplied off-white receipt feeds out.
5. **Arduino fallback.** The printer and receipt stay primary. Arduino enters as
   a smaller adjacent fallback board, joined with a restrained physical cable and
   one small indicator—never as the primary path.

## Lighting hierarchy

A permanent cool-neutral ambient, hemisphere and front fill preserve material
information. Animated archive key/rim, recognition key/webcam rim and printer
key/rim focus attention without swapping rigs. Recognition acknowledgement is a
brief cool local light around the active token and webcam; thermal warmth remains
local to the slot and paper. Black hardware is separated by believable reflected
edges, never neon emissive outlines.

## Text language

Google Font **VT323** is the machine-interface voice for statuses, theme words,
project terms and receipt-machine text. A single high-contrast upper-centre status
line is 24–34px on desktop and 20–26px on mobile. It crossfades and rises a few
pixels, while only short, meaningful fragments appear below it. Long explanation,
fake code and static caption blocks are avoided.

## Prohibited patterns

- black voids, grey modelling viewports, floor splits, CSS canvas darkness;
- cards, boxes, panels, dashboards, borders or generic AI-portfolio motifs;
- scan bars, grids, arcs, holograms, cones, fake lens overlays or neon bloom;
- paper-roll models, abrupt conditional scene replacement and random spinning;
- tiny low-contrast labels, typewriter/status flashing or long model-covering text.
