# Motion Lab — acceptance checklist

- [ ] All nine required GLBs are mounted: book, owl, snake, rose, crown, star,
  webcam, receipt printer and Arduino.
- [ ] `paper-roll.glb` is not loaded or displayed in `/motion-lab`.
- [ ] Archive/training shows book plus five identifiable theme objects in a shallow arc.
- [ ] Webcam presents its physical lens toward incoming star and rose, in a readable
  three-quarter view rather than its branded rear face.
- [ ] Star and rose take continuous, reversible curved approaches; no pose switching occurs.
- [ ] Recognition uses only controlled local glow and status confirmation—no scan/UI overlays.
- [ ] Dark exhibition atmosphere is preserved while black webcam/printer materials remain legible.
- [ ] Inactive archive objects recede before the printer becomes hero.
- [ ] Printer enters only after recognition clears; slot, rim and warm thermal practical are visible.
- [ ] Receipt emerges from the printer slot, curls slightly, and its authentic text is gated by feed.
- [ ] Arduino enters last as a secondary fallback beside the printer, with a physical cable.
- [ ] VT323 is used for all machine/status/receipt text at readable desktop and mobile sizes.
- [ ] Forward and reverse scroll reverse transforms, lights, fragments and receipt feed together.
- [ ] Desktop and mobile have no clipping, horizontal overflow, dominant developer UI or model clutter.
- [ ] Reduced-motion retains a calm, understandable final staged tableau.
